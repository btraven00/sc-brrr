PCA → kNN → Leiden on single-cell data. Lower is better everywhere. Times are per replicate, in seconds: the median over same-seed and different-seed replicates; warm-up is the one-off cold start (imports, JIT, GPU init). Growth: memory added per replicate (MB). Device comes from the entry's declared capabilities. Bloat (env size, packages) is shown, not ranked. Fidelity gate not computed yet: every entry is listed. Every plan is shown together; pick one plan to see only its results. Baselines run on a schedule, and their row pools every replicate of every run (their spread includes the spread over runs). Click a column to sort.
